Explanations for Arsenic 
Instances of arsenic contamination are highest 
near hydraulic fracturing sites and in shallower 
water wells 
• Direct contamination not likely (e.g., flowback spills or 
faulty casing)  
• Indirect causes 
• Scale/rust in wells – Increased pH and vibrations 
• Lowered water table – Shallow wells shallower
